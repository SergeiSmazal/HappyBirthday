在天愿作比翼鸟，
在地愿为连理枝。
天长地久有时尽，
此恨绵绵无绝期。

In heaven, we wish to be birds flying wing to wing,
On earth, we wish to be trees with intertwined branches.
Even if heaven and earth may one day pass away,
Our love and this depth of feeling shall never end.

-- "Song of Everlasting Regret" by Bai Juyi --
